# Sharpen the three-audience message

## What the site says today

The site already targets three groups — Distributors, Decorators, Referral Partners — with a homepage section and a dedicated page for each. So the structure is right. The pain points are only partly there.

**Distributors** — covered: too many stores on too many platforms, forwarding POs all day, drowning in "where's my order", everyone frustrated. Missing: having to build and maintain those client sites themselves, keeping pricing current, catalog management, and juggling ordering across many different suppliers and vendors.

**Decorators** — covered: every PO in a different format, manual data entry, double-checking, overwhelmed by status calls. Missing: POs that arrive incomplete, and the receptionist's full range (returns and support issues, taking messages, transferring calls).

**Referral Partners** — the weakest fit. It reads as a general "earn commissions" pitch. Missing: entrepreneurs as an audience, up to 40% commissions on apparel and on monthly recurring subscriptions, and the acquisition stack you give them (full custom acquisition website, AI voice, AI chat, AI social, AI SEO, AI reputation).

**Across all three** — the AI receptionist is described narrowly as "answers phone calls for order status". It should consistently say: answers calls and texts, gives order tracking and product info, handles support issues like returns, takes messages, transfers calls, and manages inbound Facebook and Instagram Messenger. Also missing everywhere: we connect to every supplier and to nearly any decorator, including the ones still running on spreadsheets.

## What to change

1. **Homepage persona cards** — rewrite the three summaries so each leads with that group's pain, not a generic benefit.

2. **For Distributors page** — add pain cards for building/maintaining client sites, pricing and catalog upkeep, and multi-supplier ordering. Add a solution card for universal supplier and decorator connectivity (including spreadsheet-based decorators). Expand the AI Voice card to the full receptionist capability list.

3. **For Decorators page** — add "POs arrive incomplete" to the pain list. Expand the AI Voice card the same way, and make Facebook and Instagram Messenger explicit in the support card.

4. **For Referral Partners page** — add entrepreneurs to the audience row, add a commissions block stating up to 40% on apparel and on monthly recurring subscriptions, and add a section covering the acquisition stack partners receive (custom acquisition website, AI voice, AI chat, AI social, AI SEO, AI reputation).

5. **Page metadata** — update the three persona page titles and descriptions to match the sharpened pain points. The decorator description currently talks about "artwork QA and proofing", which is not what the page says.

## Decision needed

Project memory says to keep specific pricing off the marketing site and route to "Book a Demo". The 40% commission figure is a payout, not a price, so the plan includes it as written — say the word if you'd rather it read "industry-leading commissions" instead.

## Scope

Copy and content only, inside the three persona pages, the homepage persona cards, and their SEO tags. No layout, color, component, or routing changes.
